Implementation path

From proof of concept to internal pilot.

No production deployment is proposed until access control, privacy, security and company policies are reviewed.

PHASE 01 · NOW

Demonstration

  • Interactive local prototype
  • Employee and manager workflows
  • Feedback from directors and staff
PHASE 02 · VALIDATION

Controlled pilot

  • Confirmed requirements and HR rules
  • Identity, roles and backend
  • Multi-user tests and audit trail
  • Security and privacy review
PHASE 03 · DECISION

Production readiness

  • Hosting, support and backup plan
  • Migration and Microsoft 365 decisions
  • Cost and risk comparison
  • Management sign-off
Proposed next step

Approve a small discovery pilot—not a full replacement yet.

Identify the two or three workflows that would save the most administrative time. Validate them with a small cross-functional group before deciding whether to replace any current services.
