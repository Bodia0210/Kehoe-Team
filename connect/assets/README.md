# Company logo sources

The application displays the original logos from the companies' official websites. Original proportions and colours are preserved. The Midwest logo is the official version for a dark background.

- **Kehoe Equipment Ltd.** — [official website](https://www.kel-hvac.com/). The PNG is the 2× header-logo asset served by the company's Wix image CDN: `421ec4_2ee8df0dec1444f38a5a35c6f7d02349~mv2.png`, 654 × 212 pixels.
- **Midwest Engineering (AB) Ltd.** — [official website](https://www.midwesteng.ab.ca/). Original SVG: [midwest-logo.svg](https://www.midwesteng.ab.ca/themes/midwest/images/midwest-logo.svg), viewBox 0 0 202 75.

The logos identify the companies in this prototype; they are company trademarks and are not part of a newly designed combined logo.
